TX HVAC: Google Search Ads Campaign Setup

Set up a Google Search Ads campaign from scratch for a residential HVAC client, with the goal of turning search traffic into real, qualified leads.

Using Google Ads' Keyword Planner, I built a keyword research framework covering 50+ keywords across AC Repair and Heating Services, scoring each by search intent, competition level, trending growth, and bid cost. Keywords were prioritized into "Launch Now" versus "Scale Later" tiers, so ad spend targeted the highest-intent, most cost-effective searches first.

B2B Lead Generation: Structured Cold Outreach System

Most prospect lists are unfiltered, incomplete, or fail to target the right decision-makers, leading to wasted outreach and low response rates. The goal was to build a targeted B2B prospect list focused specifically on relevant decision-makers (CEOs, Founders, Managers) at companies worth targeting for cold outreach.

I used LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify prospects by industry, job title, and company type, then manually reviewed each profile to confirm it matched the ideal customer profile. I used Apollo.io to enrich each contact, finding verified professional email addresses and validating additional details.

Result: 150 qualified B2B prospects, built and verified through LinkedIn Sales Navigator targeting, manual qualification, and Apollo.io enrichment, ready for cold outreach with accurate contact information.

Business names, contact names, emails, and website URLs blurred to protect client and prospect privacy.
